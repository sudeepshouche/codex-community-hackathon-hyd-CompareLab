## Design Context

### Users
Primary user is a marketing lead at a 20–200 person company or a solo founder. They are choosing between two short video ads before committing budget. They need a fast, credible read on which creative is more likely to hold attention and land more effectively, without needing to understand neuroscience or model internals.

### Brand Personality
Smooth, simple, credible. The interface should feel calm, productized, and operationally trustworthy rather than experimental or academic. It should help a user move from upload to decision with very little friction.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-mode, high-density SaaS UI with progressive disclosure. Reference feel comes from Firecrawl, Vercel, and OpenAI products: strong structure, restrained surfaces, low-friction interactions, plain language, and technical depth hidden behind clean defaults. Anti-direction: research-demo clutter, loud data-viz dashboards, faux-neuroscience theatrics, dark glossy “AI” styling, and overwhelming jargon.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the decision, not the model. The first screen after analysis should answer what stands out and why.
2. Use progressive disclosure consistently. Default to a simple business readout; place technical metrics and diagnostics behind expandable detail.
3. Keep interaction cost low. Every step should feel obvious, quick, and recoverable, especially for first-time users.
4. Make the UI feel productized, not demo-like. Tight hierarchy, crisp copy, and stable layout matter more than decorative flourish.
5. Preserve trust through clarity. Brain views and model outputs should be labeled honestly as predicted/schematic, not medical fact.
